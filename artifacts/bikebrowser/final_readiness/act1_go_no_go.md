# Act 1 — GO / NO-GO (Executive Brain)

> The only question that matters now: **"Would someone enjoy Act 1 enough to want
> Act 2?"**

## Decision: **A — RELEASE FOR EXTERNAL PLAYTEST NOW.**

### Why GO (not "polish first")
1. **It's stable and complete.** Build clean; smoke + full Act-1 progression with
   save/resume + bridge-reachability + all new-content drivers (side quests, Community
   Crossing) pass. The one real softlock (earn_trust) was fixed. Nothing crashes or
   traps the player.
2. **It's emotionally legible.** For an engaged player, the arc lands — the testing
   thesis, the character heart-beats, and a genuinely strong climax (Mr. Chen's
   wordless pride; Zuzu's "the bridge was never the point; the crossing was").
3. **The open questions are playtest questions.** The #1 risk — *do players engage
   with the optional emotional content, and does the crossing land?* — **cannot be
   answered by polishing; only by watching real players.** Polishing first would mean
   solving guessed problems. A playtest is precisely the instrument for this.
4. **Art is demo-grade, not broken.** No art issue is Critical (see
   `art_priority_review.md`); the package discloses it honestly.

### What would have made it NO-GO (none are true)
- A crash/softlock on the main path. ❌ (none)
- The climax unreadable or unreachable. ❌ (reachable, readable)
- Inability to complete Act 1. ❌ (validated)

### Pre-playtest actions (low-risk, freeze-safe — recommended, not blocking)
- Apply playtest-package text improvements #1–#3 (onboarding note + A/B play framing +
  engagement-tracking questions) — these **sharpen the signal on the #1 risk**.
- (Optional hygiene) remove the 8 dangling draft manifest entries.
None of these block the playtest; they improve what we learn from it.

### What to watch in feedback (decides the next phase)
- Did players engage optional content (NPCs, notebook, side quests)? → if no, the
  emotional design needs *routing* fixes, not *writing* fixes (backlog).
- Did the Community Crossing land? Which character is remembered? Did the science feel
  natural? Where did they stall? Which art pulled them out?

### Bottom line
Act 1 is **good enough to give 20 players a real first experience and to tell us
whether they'd want Act 2.** Ship it to playtest; let the data drive the next pass.
**Do not expand scope.** Optimize for experience, not content.
