# Character Memory Test (Executive Brain)

Test: **"What would a player remember about this character 24 hours later?"** —
*assuming they engaged the optional content.* The parenthetical caveat is itself the
headline finding: memorability is gated behind optional dialogue.

## Zuzu (player) — STRONG (if notebook is read)
- **Remembered:** the kid who *tests before she trusts*; her notebook voice ("the
  bridge was never the point; the crossing was").
- **Emotional moments:** the crossing reflection; "I was scared Dex was right."
- **Weakness:** her interiority lives in the notebook (N key) — a player who never
  opens it gets a quieter Zuzu. Strong *if* read.
- **Opportunity (backlog):** surface one Zuzu reflection inline at a key beat so even
  non-notebook players feel her voice.

## Dex — STRONG-IF-ENGAGED / WEAK-IF-NOT
- **Remembered:** "the cocky 'just send it' kid who came around" — calls Zuzu "brain."
- **Emotional moments:** dex_heart (the armor: measured once, it broke, everyone
  laughed) → the kindness branch → his care at the crossing.
- **Weakness:** his whole arc is optional + multi-visit. A player who ignores him
  remembers a green-tinted clone who said one cocky line. **Highest variance** of any
  character. (Also the tinted-clone sprite hurts memorability — art review.)
- **Opportunity (backlog):** ensure at least his intro + the crossing beat are hard
  to miss.

## Mr. Chen — MODERATE-STRONG
- **Remembered:** the calm mentor; "evidence before confidence." With the heart-beat,
  the porch-beam regret is genuinely memorable.
- **Emotional moments:** mr_chen_heart (the family's faces); his wordless pride at the
  crossing.
- **Weakness:** without the heart-beat he's a wise-mentor archetype. The crossing
  pride beat is silent — powerful if the player has bonded, neutral if not.

## Mrs. Ramirez — MODERATE (carries Mateo)
- **Remembered:** the warm "mija" neighbor; **Mateo** (if met).
- **Emotional moments:** ramirez_heart (Mateo on the highway road); her relief at the
  crossing ("I can breathe again").
- **Weakness:** Mateo — the emotional fulcrum of the whole climax — exists *only* in
  her optional heart-beat until he appears at the crossing. If unmet, her relief
  lands on a stranger.

## Auntie Mariam — MODERATE
- **Remembered:** the gentle Arabic mentor; **the seeds from home** (if heard).
- **Emotional moments:** mariam_heart (seeds across an ocean); carrying them across at
  the crossing; her builder past.
- **Weakness:** her depth is optional; the cultural layer is intentionally light
  (per the human-brief guardrail) so she risks reading as "kind elder" without the
  seeds beat.

## Verdict
Every character is **memorable when engaged, thin when skipped.** The single highest
lever for character memory is **reducing the optionality of one key beat per
character** (especially Mateo's setup and Dex's arc) — but that is a *design change*
under scope freeze; it goes to `future_backlog.md` and should be **measured in
playtest first** (do players actually engage the optional content?).
