# Community Crossing Audit (Executive Brain)

The Act-1 emotional climax. Reviewed against the captured screenshots
(`visual_review_pack/02_crossing_beat_*`) and the beat script.

## Does it work?
**Yes, mechanically and emotionally.** 14 beats run, the sequence completes, figures
cross the deck, the Zuzu reflection unlocks. The six scenes are clear and well
ordered (Mateo → Ramirez relief → Mariam seeds → Dex care → Chen pride → Zuzu).

## Does it feel earned?
**Partially — conditional on engagement.** It is *fully* earned for a player who did
the testing, met the neighbors, heard the heart-beats, and helped the side quests.
For a player who rushed the main chain, it introduces several payoffs cold (most
sharply **Mateo**, who may be a stranger). The repair effort itself (gather → test →
plan → build) *is* substantial, so the bridge-as-effort is earned; it's the
*people*-payoff that varies.

## Does it create emotion?
Yes for engaged players — Ramirez's "I can breathe again," Mariam's seeds, and the
Zuzu reflection are strong. The **wordless Mr. Chen** beat is the most elegant moment
(show-don't-tell).

## Does it connect to previous quests?
Strongly to the **bridge questline** (the deck "you designed and tested" holds).
**Weakly to the optional content** it pays off — because that content is optional.

## Strongest moment
**Mr. Chen's wordless pride** ("the way a teacher looks when there is nothing left to
teach") + the **Zuzu reflection** ("the bridge was never the point; the crossing
was"). These are the lines players will quote.

## Weakest moment
**Mateo's introduction at beat 1–3.** He is the emotional fulcrum but, for many
players, a stranger at this moment. The scene compensates ("a small boy with a
backpack bigger than he is"), but the impact is setup-dependent.

## Unnecessary dialogue
Minimal. The opening beat ("the whole neighborhood comes to see") is slightly
expository but sets the staging. Dex's beat is two lines + a follow — tight. No cuts
strongly indicated.

## Missing payoff
- **Visual:** figures are colored circles on a procedural backdrop — the climax
  deserves a painted backdrop (art proposal exists; human-gated). Currently the
  *words* carry the emotion alone.
- **No callback to the player's specific bridge design** (which materials they chose)
  — a small personalization opportunity (backlog).

## Verdict
A genuinely good climax whose **ceiling is high and whose floor depends on prior
engagement**. The fix for the floor (make Mateo/relationships less skippable) is a
design change → backlog + measure in playtest. The visual lift is art part B
(human-gated). **As-is, it is playtest-ready and emotionally legible.**
