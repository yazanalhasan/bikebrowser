# Playtest Package Review (Executive Brain)

Reviewed `playtest_package/README.md`. It is solid (run steps, what to test, known
issues, feedback questions, target experience, checklist). Improvements below target
the **#1 risk (engagement-gated emotion)** and onboarding — all *instructional*, no
game changes.

## Strengths
- Honest known-issues list (Dex sprite, placeholder art, dangling entries).
- Feedback questions already probe the right things (why the bridge mattered, which
  character, STEM-natural-or-forced, crossing-as-payoff, art, confusion).

## Recommended improvements (to the package text, not the game)
1. **Add a "first 3 minutes" onboarding note:** "Press E near the glowing markers;
   follow the quest trail + GPS (top-left says where to go next)." Reduces early
   "what do I do" friction without changing the game.
2. **Two playthrough framings:** ask half the testers to play *however they like*
   (measures natural engagement with optional content) and half to *talk to every
   NPC and open the notebook* (measures the intended emotional ceiling). This
   A/B framing is the single most valuable change — it directly measures the #1 risk.
3. **Add engagement-tracking questions:** "Did you talk to Dex more than once? Did you
   open the notebook? Did you do either side quest?" — so we can correlate
   "the ending didn't land" with "skipped the setups."
4. **Set expectation on art:** one line — "Art is demo-grade; some characters/
   backgrounds are placeholders. Tell us which ones pulled you out, not just that
   they're rough."
5. **Add a 'golden path' time estimate** (~20–30 min) so testers allot time.

## Verdict
The package is **playtest-ready**; applying improvements #1–#3 (text-only) would
materially sharpen the feedback signal on the project's biggest open question. These
are documentation edits, allowed under the freeze.
