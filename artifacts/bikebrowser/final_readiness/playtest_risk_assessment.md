# Playtest Risk Assessment (Executive Brain)

Predicted new-player feedback, with probability × impact × mitigation. Impact = how
much it threatens "would they want Act 2?"

| Likely feedback | Prob | Impact | Why | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"I didn't realize the emotional stuff was there / the ending didn't hit"** | **High** | **High** | heart-beats, Mateo, Dex's arc, side quests are all optional; rushers miss the setups | playtest *measures* this; if confirmed, reduce optionality of 1 beat/character (backlog). Package nudges players to talk to NPCs + open the notebook |
| **"The art looks unfinished"** | **High** | Med | flat ground, Dex clone, circle-figures | disclosed in playtest package as demo-grade; collect *which* assets hurt most |
| "I wasn't sure what to do at first" | Med | Med | dense first screen; the loop (talk→find→gather→test→plan→build) isn't tutorialized | GPS/quest-trail/clue-box already guide; watch where players stall |
| "The testing felt shallow / clicky" | Med | Med | gather-8 / test-8 batch-complete in one action → the UTM thesis is told, not felt | candidate de-pad (backlog); measure if players notice |
| **"The Community Crossing felt great"** | Med-High | High (positive) | the climax is strong for engaged players | confirm the strongest moments (Chen pride, Zuzu line) land |
| "Dex annoyed me" | Low-Med | Low-Med | he's a cocky foil by design | the kindness branch + his arc soften him; gauge tone reception |
| "The science felt natural / I learned without being taught" | Med | High (positive) | relationships-not-facts design | validate the core thesis lands |
| "I got stuck / softlocked" | Low | High | earn_trust softlock fixed (P0); full path validated | none expected; watch for edge cases the scripted tests miss |
| "Performance was rough on load" | Low | Low | large first-load chunks | not in-game; note only if raised |

## Headline
The **dominant risk is engagement-gated emotion** (High×High): the game's best
content is optional, so a meaningful fraction of players may experience a competent
quest game without the heart that makes it memorable. **This is the #1 thing the
playtest must measure** (track: did players talk to all NPCs? open the notebook?
do the side quests?). Everything else is expected demo feedback.
