# BikeBrowser Relationship Bible — Act 1 (Executive Brain)

The block is a **web of relationships**, not a list of quest-givers. This is the
emotional engine. The unifying theme: **trust is earned by evidence — between people
as well as bridges** ("test before you trust" → "you trust a bridge because it held;
maybe that's how Dex started trusting me," zuzu_trust).

## The web
```
            Mr. Chen ──(two builders who value proof)── Auntie Mariam
               │                                            │
        (steady teacher)                            (mentor / sees herself in Zuzu)
               │                                            │
               └──────────────► ZUZU ◄───────────────────-─┘
                                 ▲  │
            (protector of block) │  │ (rivalry → respect → friendship)
                                 │  ▼
                          Mrs. Ramirez            Dex
```

## Pairwise canon
- **Zuzu ↔ Mr. Chen** — mentee/mentor. He hands her instruments, never lectures
  (the UTM pattern as relationship). His porch-beam regret teaches her that evidence
  is *care*.
- **Zuzu ↔ Auntie Mariam** — Mariam sees her younger builder-self in Zuzu; pride and
  belonging. The garden quest deepens this (helping home put down roots).
- **Zuzu ↔ Mrs. Ramirez** — warm/maternal ("mija"). Through Mateo, Zuzu feels the
  human stakes of the repair.
- **Zuzu ↔ Dex** — the central dynamic arc: rivalry (bravado) → grudging respect
  (the tested bridge holds) → vulnerability + tentative partnership ("we test the
  next one, together"). Zuzu's kindness (not winning) is what turns it.
- **Mrs. Ramirez ↔ Auntie Mariam** — neighbors across the wash; Mariam's garden is
  on Ramirez's "who depends on this crossing" list. Their bond makes the block feel
  lived-in.
- **Mr. Chen ↔ Auntie Mariam** — two builders, two cultures, one ethic: proof before
  trust. (Light-touch; expand only with care.)

## Rules for new relationship content
1. **Trust is earned by evidence/care**, never declared. Show the proof, then the
   trust.
2. **No new antagonists** beyond Dex's friendly rivalry — the tone is warm community,
   not conflict. Dex is a foil, not a villain.
3. **Every relationship reinforces a STEM value** by living it (Chen=care-in-rigor,
   Ramirez=patience-is-kind, Mariam=observation/continuity, Dex=prediction-earned).
4. Mateo, the school, the mercado, the garden are **shared anchors** — reference
   them to keep the world coherent.
