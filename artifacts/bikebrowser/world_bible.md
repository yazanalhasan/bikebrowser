# BikeBrowser World Bible — Act 1 (Executive Brain)

Canon for setting. **Every new quest must fit this world.** Source: arc.md §Act 1 +
the shipped `src/game/phaser/` Neighborhood/scene content.

## Setting
The **Sonoran Desert** (Phoenix/Tempe/Scottsdale-inspired Arizona). Local, grounded,
resource-constrained. A kid's neighborhood split in two by a **dry wash** whose
footbridge washed out in a monsoon flood. Warm, sunlit, dusty, hopeful.

## Locations (Act 1, current)
- **The street / neighborhood (hub):** homes (adobe/pueblo), the social heart of the
  block. Where Zuzu, Mr. Chen, Mrs. Ramirez, Auntie Mariam, and now **Dex** are met.
- **The garage / workbench:** Mr. Chen's; the conceptual home base and tool home
  (materials, UTM access). Carries forward across all acts (arc.md).
- **The dry wash & broken crossing:** the central problem. A monsoon channel that
  floods fast and runs dry; the footbridge needs a *proven* repair.
- **The materials yard / UTM rig:** where the 8 materials (balsa, pine, bamboo,
  brick, concrete, iron, steel, carbon fiber) are gathered and tested.
- **The ecology patch:** mesquite, creosote, saguaro — desert plants observed
  respectfully (harvest ethics: "observe first, take little, leave habitat").
- **The chemistry station:** mix/dry/test a sealant for the repaired deck.
- **The wider-map gate:** opens after the crossing is reconnected — the Act-1 → Act-2
  hook (a sketch of a wheel, a bridge, and "something shaped like a tiny spacecraft
  frame").
- **Across the wash (world-building, light-touch):** the **school** (where Mateo
  crosses), the **mercado** (Mrs. Ramirez's errands), and **Auntie Mariam's garden**
  (the saguaro from home). These give the crossing human weight.

## History & environmental storytelling
- A **monsoon flood** scoured the bridge footings (not just "weak planks" — the
  evidence is a high-water mark + a scour hole). The real fix is deeper, protected
  footings. (Notebook: wash_scour, wash_water.)
- A **dry wash is not lifeless** — deep mesquite roots reach subsurface moisture.
- The block is multi-cultural and interdependent: English/Spanish/Arabic neighbors,
  a shared crossing, a shared stake in getting it right.

## Community relationships (world layer)
The crossing is the literal and emotional connective tissue: kids to school, elders
to the mercado, Mariam's garden of home — all depend on it. The repair is not a
chore; it's the block trusting each other through proof. (See relationship_bible.)

## Future regions (arc.md roadmap — NOT Act 1 scope)
Act 1 grows overland (e-bike → motorcycle → car) before Act 2's sea/air regions and
Act 3's alien planet. Six Earth biomes with real cultures/languages/resources feed
the seven-vehicle spine. **Do not build these now** — Act 1 / Chapter 1 (Sonoran
bike) is the ship target. Anything beyond is roadmap, tagged accordingly.

## World rules (arc.md §8 — must honor)
- **No biomes/landmarks without an educational domain.** Every place teaches.
- **Terrain is a constraint engine, not decoration** (the wash floods; the sun
  scorches; soil washes downhill).
- **Cultural authenticity, not decoration**; specific cultural content needs a human
  brief.
- **Stewardship over extraction** (harvest ethics, observe-first ecology).
