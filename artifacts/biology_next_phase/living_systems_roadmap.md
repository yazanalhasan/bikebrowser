# Living Systems Roadmap — first desert plant to terraforming

Verifies the complete biology pathway stays coherent end to end.

## Naming reconciliation (resolves audit §3)
The request listed "Act 1–5." But the game has **three acts by medium**
(Ground / Sea & Air / Space) and **seven vehicle chapters** — that is
fixed canon (arc.md §3). So the biology arc is named here as **Biology
Phases BP1–BP5**, an overlay **mapped onto** the existing acts/chapters,
**not** new game acts. (Three axes: game acts = medium; vehicle chapters
= engineering; biology phases = living-systems depth. They compose.)

```
BP1 Living Systems        → Act 1 (Ground), Chapters 1–2  (bike, e-bike)
BP2 Cells & Molecules     → Act 1–2,         Chapters 3–4  (motorcycle, car)
BP3 Biological Engineering→ Act 2–3,         Chapters 5–6  (boat, plane)
BP4 Life Support Systems  → Act 3 (Space),   Chapter 7     (spacecraft)
BP5 Terraforming Ecology  → Act 3 (Space),   Chapter 7 capstone (alien planet)
```

Cognitive overlay (Educational Hierarchy): BP1 Observe → BP2 Explain →
BP3 Predict→Engineer → BP4–BP5 Engineer→Teach.

## BP1 — Living Systems (Act 1 · Ch 1–2 · Observe)
- **Scientific focus:** ecology, plants, animals, adaptation, ethnobotany;
  food-web edges (who pollinates/eats/nurses whom).
- **Engineering focus:** mechanical + first electrical; plant materials
  (fiber, wood, charcoal, wax).
- **Gameplay focus:** exploration, scanning, crafting, food-web discovery;
  Sonoran desert.
- **Reasoning focus:** pattern_recognition, spatial, causal,
  experimental_design.
- **Major unlocks:** Microscope (end of phase, preview), Extraction Bench
  (Ch2 entry), the food-web map. Instruments owned: UTM, Growth Chamber
  (intro).

## BP2 — Cells & Molecules (Act 1–2 · Ch 3–4 · Explain)
- **Scientific focus:** phytochemistry (plant→compound), cellular biology
  (microscope: cells, organelles, CAM), molecular preview (pathways);
  microbiology begins (fermentation, soil life).
- **Engineering focus:** combustion/thermal (fuels, resins), systems
  integration (car); bio-materials (cellulose, composites).
- **Gameplay focus:** investigation, experimentation; "why does this
  work?" at the cell/molecule scale.
- **Reasoning focus:** causal, evidence_evaluation, model_building,
  feedback_loop (intro).
- **Major unlocks:** Pharmacology Bench (mechanism investigation),
  Fermentation Bench, full Microscope + Molecular Mode. The Knowledge
  Graph becomes navigable molecule↔organism.

## BP3 — Biological Engineering (Act 2–3 · Ch 5–6 · Predict→Engineer)
- **Scientific focus:** systems biology (networks), selective breeding,
  microbial engineering, synthetic biology (in simulation); genetics/
  genomics/evolution domains.
- **Engineering focus:** fluid/aero (boat, plane); strength-to-weight
  bio-composites; the simulators.
- **Gameplay focus:** simulation-first design; Trait/Population/Genome
  Simulators; "predict the cascade before you act."
- **Reasoning focus:** systems_thinking, tradeoff_analysis,
  uncertainty_reasoning, model_building.
- **Major unlocks:** Genetics Workbench (DNA→RNA→Protein→Trait), Trait
  Simulator, Population Simulator, Genome Simulator. **The
  prediction-precedes-intervention gate becomes the core loop.**

## BP4 — Life Support Systems (Act 3 · Ch 7 · Engineer)
- **Scientific focus:** closed-loop ecology; mass/energy balance;
  redundancy; which organisms make oxygen, fix nitrogen, recycle water/
  waste, provide calories.
- **Engineering focus:** vacuum/life-support; spacecraft & habitat loops.
- **Gameplay focus:** assemble a closed loop from mastered biology; test
  under failure (a crop dies → predict cascade).
- **Reasoning focus:** feedback_loop_reasoning, tradeoff_analysis,
  systems_thinking, uncertainty.
- **Major unlocks:** Ecosystem Simulator at habitat scale; the mandatory
  release pipeline (Simulation → Containment → Small-scale → Ecosystem
  review → Release).

## BP5 — Terraforming Ecology (Act 3 capstone · Ch 7 · Engineer→Teach)
- **Scientific focus:** ecological succession, carrying capacity,
  resilience, reversibility, containment; planetary closed-loop ecology.
- **Engineering focus:** planetary-scale life systems; same schema as
  Earth (no alien carve-out).
- **Gameplay focus:** Terra Nil-style restoration; success = a stable,
  reversible, contained ecosystem; leave without harm; teach the system.
- **Reasoning focus:** all 14 domains converge; systems_thinking +
  feedback_loop + uncertainty dominate.
- **Major unlocks:** the capstone "stable biome" win state; Teach-level
  quests (explain the whole system so an NPC/AI can predict it).

## End-to-end coherence check (the audit's goal)
A single thread runs unbroken from Chapter 1 to terraforming:

> **Observe a desert plant** (BP1) → **understand its cells & molecules**
> (BP2) → **understand the ecosystem it lives in** (food-web) →
> **predict consequences and engineer responsibly** (BP3) → **build a
> closed life-support loop** (BP4) → **terraform a stable, reversible
> biome and teach it** (BP5).

Every step:
- reuses the **same Biology Workbench** (modes deepen; no fragmentation);
- reads the **same Knowledge Graph** (one `speciesId` gains facets);
- is gated by **prediction precedes intervention**;
- is scored toward **stewardship, not domination**;
- maps to a **vehicle chapter** (engineering) and a **grade band**
  (cognitive readiness) without contradiction.

The yucca fiber that fixes a bike bag in Chapter 1 and the nitrogen-fixer
that stabilizes an alien soil in BP5 are the **same system at different
depths** — which is exactly the design goal: biology as one strong,
interconnected pathway, not a content area.
