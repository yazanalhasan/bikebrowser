# Leonardo Bridge Engineering Quest

## Purpose

This quest is the first major engineering challenge that connects:

* Ecology
* Investigation
* Materials Science
* UTM Testing
* Engineering Design
* Community Building

The bridge is not an engineering puzzle for its own sake.

The bridge exists because:

Mr. Chen cannot visit his lifelong friend Mrs. Ramirez across the wash.

The player is solving a human problem.

---

# Educational Objectives

Player should learn:

1. Materials have different properties.
2. Compression and tension are different.
3. Strongest material is not always best.
4. Structures distribute force.
5. Engineering solves human problems.
