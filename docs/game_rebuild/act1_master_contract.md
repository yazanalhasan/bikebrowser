# Act 1 Master Contract

## Player Fantasy

Zuzu begins as a local kid with a bike, a garage, and neighbors who know the desert. The player solves real local problems by checking tools, observing the world, testing materials, building trust, and making evidence-based repairs.

## Emotional Progression

Act 1 starts with a familiar neighborhood problem: the bike and the broken dry-wash crossing. It ends with Zuzu trusted by neighbors, capable with tools, and ready to leave the local map toward a wider systems world.

## Educational Progression

Progression follows canon:

```text
repair -> construction -> experimentation -> design -> simulation -> creation
```

Act 1 covers repair, construction, experimentation, and first design confidence. Simulation and creation are seeded, not completed.

## Start State

- Zuzu is in the neighborhood.
- Bike is available but not checked.
- Garage/workbench is nearby but not fully unlocked.
- Dry wash bridge is broken.
- Map is mostly undiscovered.
- Notebook exists but is empty.

## End State

- Bike upgraded/checked.
- Bridge crossing reconnected.
- Material testing is understood.
- Ecology and chemistry are introduced.
- Trust and language layers function.
- Wider map gate is unlocked.
- First Act 2 systems/spacecraft clue is revealed.

## Cultural Rule

Spanish and Arabic interactions must be natural, contextual, and relationship-based. Deep culturally sensitive content is out of scope until a human-authored brief exists.
