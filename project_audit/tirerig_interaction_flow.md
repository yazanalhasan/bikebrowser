# TireRig Interaction Flow

Date: 2026-05-22

## Learning Goal

Player takeaway:

> Air escaped through a hole, and the patch sealed the hole.

## Flow

### 1. Feel For The Leak

Player action:

- Hold `E` near the tire station.

System response:

- Tire remains soft.
- One leak location becomes visible.
- Air escape trace appears.
- Feedback: "Air is escaping from one tiny hole."

Purpose:

- Teaches diagnosis before repair.

### 2. Mark Leak In Notebook

Player action:

- Press `E` when prompted to mark the leak.

System response:

- Notebook records a leak zone.

Purpose:

- Turns observation into an artifact.

### 3. Ease Tube Out

Player action:

- Hold `E`.

System response:

- Tube becomes visible outside the wheel.
- Hole remains in the same readable position.

Purpose:

- Teaches access: the part that holds air must be reachable before repair.

### 4. Clean, Glue, Press Patch

Player action:

- Hold `E`.

System response:

- Prepared/scuffed glue zone appears around the hole.
- Single rubber patch appears over that exact spot.
- Air escape trace fades as patch seal increases.
- Feedback: "The cleaned patch covers the hole; the hiss stops."

Purpose:

- Teaches why preparation matters and why the patch must cover the hole.

### 5. Pump Until Tire Firms

Player action:

- Hold `E`.

System response:

- Gauge needle rises.
- Tire deformation decreases.
- Feedback: "Air stays inside now, so the tire firms up."

Purpose:

- Teaches pressure returning because the leak is sealed.

### 6. Spin And Check

Player action:

- Hold `E`.

System response:

- Wheel spins only now.
- Ready glow and notebook artifact appear.

Purpose:

- Teaches verification after repair, not during diagnosis.

## Feedback Tone

Feedback stays quiet and mechanical. It should name what changed in the object, not praise the player generically.
